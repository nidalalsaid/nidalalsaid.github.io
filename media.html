Profile Overview

My research focuses on the integration of artificial intelligence into communication and media technologies, with particular emphasis on how intelligent systems transform digital content, user interaction, and information trust.

This work positions media as a data-driven and computational ecosystem, where machine learning models, user behavior, and communication processes interact to shape modern digital environments.

The research spans social media analytics, intelligent content systems, human-AI interaction, media authenticity, and emerging immersive communication environments.

Positioning Statement
