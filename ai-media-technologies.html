Profile Overview

My research focuses on the integration of artificial intelligence into communication and media technologies, with particular emphasis on how intelligent systems transform digital content, user interaction, and information trust. This work positions media as a data-driven and computational ecosystem where machine learning models, user behavior, and communication processes interact to shape modern digital environments.

Positioning Statement

Main umbrella: AI-driven communication and media technologies.

Core strengths: Social media analytics, computational media, human-computer interaction, intelligent content systems, and digital trust.

Research identity: AI for digital media, journalism, social platforms, and communication systems.
