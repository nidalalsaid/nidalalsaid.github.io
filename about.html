Dr. Nidal Al Said

Dr. Nidal Al Said is an Associate Professor of Information Technology at Ajman University in the United Arab Emirates. He has more than twenty years of academic and professional experience across Jordan, Oman, Greece, and the UAE.

His work focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Human Computer Interaction, Data Mining, and Smart Systems. His research aims to bridge the gap between advanced computational models and real-world applications in education, media, and digital environments.

Dr. Al Said has contributed to international journals and conferences, with research covering AI-driven trust, intelligent analytics, and human-centered system design. His work emphasizes both technical performance and practical usability.

Research Interests

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Human Computer Interaction, Data Mining and Analytics, Social Computing, Smart Systems, and AI applications in education and digital transformation.

Academic Contributions

Dr. Al Said is actively involved in curriculum development, accreditation processes, and institutional quality assurance. He contributes to designing modern academic programs that integrate AI technologies and industry-relevant skills.

His teaching approach focuses on clarity, applied learning, and preparing students for real-world problem solving using modern computational tools.
