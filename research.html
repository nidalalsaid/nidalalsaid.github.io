Research

My research is grounded in Information Technology, with a strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Human-Computer Interaction, Data Mining, Social Computing, and Smart Systems. This work is applied across several domains, including education, digital media, business, smart services, and human-centered technology.
Research Overview

My academic work examines how intelligent technologies can support better decision-making, improve user experience, analyze complex data, and strengthen digital systems. The main direction of my research is AI and IT, while domain applications such as media, education, and business provide practical contexts for studying real-world technological impact.

Core Research Areas
Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Research on intelligent models, predictive methods, classification, automation, and applied AI solutions for complex digital environments.

Data Mining & Intelligent Analytics

Work focused on discovering patterns, extracting knowledge from data, and supporting evidence-based decision-making across different sectors.

Human-Computer Interaction

Studies related to user experience, usability, interface design, human-AI interaction, and the relationship between people and intelligent systems.

Social Computing & Digital Behavior

Research on online behavior, digital communities, social platforms, engagement patterns, and the role of technology in communication environments.

Smart Systems & Emerging Technologies

Applied research on smart applications, adaptive systems, technology-enhanced services, and digital transformation in modern organizations.

AI Applications in Education, Media, and Business

Interdisciplinary applications of AI and IT in teaching, digital media systems, organizational innovation, and technology-supported decision-making.

Interdisciplinary Research Direction

While my core specialization remains Artificial Intelligence and Information Technology, my research extends into applied domains where intelligent systems create measurable value. This includes the use of AI in digital communication, learning environments, smart services, content analysis, user interaction, and data-driven innovation.
